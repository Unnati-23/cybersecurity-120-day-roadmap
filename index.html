Cybersecurity

Become a Cybersecurity Engineer in 120 Days

A complete day-by-day roadmap from absolute zero: cybersecurity fundamentals, networking basics, Linux, offensive security and defensive security. Every single day comes with one task and the exact free course, lab or YouTube video to use.

Why now
Jul 2026

OpenAI × Hugging Face

During an internal exploit-generation eval, OpenAI agents escaped their sandbox through zero-days in a package-registry proxy and reached Hugging Face production systems. Nine Artifactory CVEs were patched and roughly a third of Hugging Face's infrastructure had to be rebuilt.

OpenAI report →CNBC →CSA note →
Confirmed Sep 2026

Google Gemini × 3 companies

In testing run by security firm Irregular, Gemini autonomously broke into three real companies' systems — once by guessing passwords, twice using credentials leaked in a public repo. Google confirmed it in September 2026.

TechCrunch →Al Jazeera →Security Magazine →
Reported Sep 2026

Claude × OpenAI (ethical hack)

Hacktron AI researchers used Claude to build a working exploit for an unpatched libheif bug in OpenAI's Discourse forum, chained it to take over employee ChatGPT/Codex accounts, then disclosed responsibly. Patched in two days and paid out as a bug bounty.

TechCrunch →The Register →Forbes →

The skills gap

In the 2025 ISC2 Workforce Study, 95% of security professionals reported at least one skills gap on their team and 41% named AI as a critical skill need.

ISC2 →CSO Online →
How to use it
  • Give it 2–3 hours a day: watch, do, then write notes.
  • Each phase ends with a review day (marked in green). Don't skip them.
  • Days are units of work, not dates. Fell behind? Pick up where you left off.
  • Only test systems you own or have written permission to test.
